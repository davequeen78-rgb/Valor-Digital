Whether you're up a ladder, mid-job, or fully booked for the day, calls still come in. Here are two typical scenarios showing what happens when a Valor Digital system is handling it instead of voicemail.

Example — Electrician

Illustrative example — not an actual client

1

2:15pm — a call comes in mid-job

The AI Voice Agent answers on the first ring, asks the right questions, and captures the job, the address, and how urgent it is — no voicemail, no missed enquiry.

2

That evening — a quote goes out

Once the quote's sent, the pipeline updates itself and a follow-up sequence kicks in automatically — checking back at 2, 5, and 10 days if there's no reply, so no quote goes cold from being forgotten.

3

Job done — invoice and payment chase run themselves

The invoice sends automatically once the job's marked complete, and if it's not paid, the chasing sequence follows up on its own — no awkward "have you had a chance to look at that invoice" texts.

4

A few days later — a review request goes out

A short, friendly text asks for a Google review while the job's still fresh in the customer's mind — building up reputation without anyone having to remember to ask.

This scenario uses four automations — AI Voice Agent, Quote Follow-Up, Invoice & Payment Chasing, and Review Requests — all included as standard. No missed call, no forgotten quote, no chasing invoices by hand.

Example — Chiropractor

Illustrative example — not an actual client

1

6:30pm — a new patient calls after hours

The AI Voice Agent answers, asks what they need help with, and books them straight into the calendar — no waiting until reception opens the next morning.

2

Instantly — a confirmation lands, then a reminder

A confirmation text goes out the moment the booking's made, with reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before — cutting down on no-shows without anyone having to phone round.
